Convert

Drop a performance (the melody, timing and delivery to keep) and a target voice (1–30 s of the voice it should sound like). Works best on clean vocals, so split songs first.

Slow without an NVIDIA GPU: on an M2 Mac a 30-second clip took about 10 minutes at 30 steps. Fewer steps is faster and rougher.

Sync

Drop a song and a recording of you singing it. Your singing is lined up with the song, phrase by phrase, and mixed over the instrumental. It matches by melody, so any language works, and so does a different key or octave. It never speeds up or slows down your singing within a phrase.
