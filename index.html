Vocal tools

Everything runs on this machine. Nothing is uploaded anywhere else.

Split

Drop a song (mp3, wav, m4a, flac…) to get the vocals and the instrumental.

Drop a file here, or click to choose

Convert

Drop a performance (the melody, timing and delivery to keep) and a target voice (1–30 s of the voice it should sound like). Works best on clean vocals, so split songs first.
